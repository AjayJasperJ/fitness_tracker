Progressive overload

For dumbbell chest press, warm up lightly, then aim for 2 working sets of 8–12 controlled reps. When both sets reach 12 reps pain-free, consider a small weight increase. Do not force extra reps to reach a number.

Stop exercises that cause shoulder joint pain. If pain returns, seek qualified assessment.
